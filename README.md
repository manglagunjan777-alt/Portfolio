## 🚀 Projects

### 1. 🛍️ Diwali Sales Analysis

**Objective:**
Analyze Diwali sales data to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify high-performing customer segments and product categories, and generate insights that can support better marketing and sales decisions.

**Dataset:**
The dataset contains customer and transaction-level information including gender, age group, marital status, state, zone, occupation, product category, number of orders, and purchase amount. The analysis contains **11,251 records and 15 columns**.

**Tools & Technologies:**

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

**Key Analysis:**

* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Missing-value identification
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Customer demographic analysis
* Gender and age-group analysis
* State and zone-wise sales analysis
* Occupation-wise purchasing behavior
* Product-category analysis
* Order and sales-amount analysis
* Data visualization

**Key Takeaway:**
The analysis was used to identify the customer segments, geographic regions, occupations, and product categories that contribute most strongly to sales, providing a basis for targeted marketing and inventory decisions.

### 2. ✈️ Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

**Objective:**
Analyze airline passenger data to understand the factors associated with passenger satisfaction and identify areas where the airline can improve the overall customer experience.

**Dataset:**
The dataset includes passenger demographics and travel information such as gender, customer type, age, type of travel, travel class, flight distance, and ratings for different airline services.

**Tools & Technologies:**

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

**Key Analysis:**

* Data loading and preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis
* Passenger demographic analysis
* Travel type and class analysis
* Flight-distance analysis
* Service-rating analysis
* Passenger satisfaction analysis
* Data visualization
* Business interpretation

**Key Finding:**
The analysis indicated that a significant portion of passengers were **dissatisfied or neutral** with their flight experience, highlighting opportunities for airlines to focus on service quality and passenger experience.
